# **App Name**: Attendance App

## Core Features:

- Employee Login: Allows employees to log in using their name and employee ID, checking against approved status.
- Attendance Submission: Enables employees to submit attendance with shift, site, photo capture, GPS coordinates, date, and time.
- Geolocation and Address: Automatically captures GPS coordinates and reverse geocodes to determine the employee's address at the time of attendance submission.
- HR Dashboard: Provides HR personnel access to employee details and attendance records.
- Employee Management: Allows HR to add, delete, and manage employee status (Approved/Pending).
- Attendance Tracking and Visualization: Displays attendance records in a table format, including name, ID, shift, site, date, time, GPS, address, and photo.
- Intrusion Detection: When an employee captures their image, this tool runs an analysis of the liveness of the face as well as checking whether or not they are using AI-generated enhancements.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Navy blue (#3B5998) to convey professionalism and trust.
- Background color: Light gray (#F0F2F5) to ensure a clean and modern interface.
- Accent color: Orange (#FF7F50) to highlight key actions and interactive elements.
- Body and headline font: 'Inter' sans-serif font, providing a clean, modern, neutral, and readable appearance.
- Use consistent, minimalist icons for navigation and actions, focusing on clarity and ease of understanding.
- Maintain a clean and structured layout with clear separation of sections to enhance usability.
- Subtle transitions and feedback animations for user interactions to improve the overall experience.
